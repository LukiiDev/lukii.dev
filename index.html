Resell Bros↗

resellerbros.pages.dev

This was my first real project. It's a shop site for a reselling brand where you can browse what's available and order without any checkout.

You pick something, hit Buy, and Instagram DM opens with a message about that product already copied. You paste it, send it, and sort out payment and pickup in chat. Simple for the seller, simple for the buyer.

I also added a little game where you catch real drops and avoid the fakes. Catch enough and you get a 10% discount code. It wasn't needed, but it made the site more fun.
